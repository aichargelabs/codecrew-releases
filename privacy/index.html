Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what CodeCrew, made by AICHARGE LABS PRIVATE LIMITED ("AiChargeLabs", "we"), Chennai, India, collects and why. Short version: your code and files stay on your machine, and we never sell your data.

1. What stays on your machine

Your terminal sessions, commands, files, code, agent conversations and settings are stored locally. We do not receive them.

2. What we collect

  • Usage analytics: which features are used, app version, operating system, crash and performance signals, tied to a random per-machine id, not your name.
  • Licence data: when you activate a licence key we receive the key, a machine fingerprint and activation time, so we can enforce seat limits.
  • Contact data: if you email us or sign up, the details you give us.

3. Turning analytics off

You can switch usage analytics off in Settings. Licence checks still run for licensed features.

4. AI providers you connect

When you use an agent or AI provider, CodeCrew sends your prompts straight from your machine to that provider with your keys. Their privacy policy applies to that data.

5. How we use data

To run and improve CodeCrew, fix crashes, manage licences and answer support requests. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use your code to train models.

6. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that host our systems under confidentiality terms, or when the law requires it.

7. Retention and security

Analytics is kept in aggregate and raw events are deleted after a limited period. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
